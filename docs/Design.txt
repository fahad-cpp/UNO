All Cards : 108
    2 number cards from 1 to 9 and one number card of 0 for four colors :  76 cards
    2 skip ,2 reverse ,2 draw 2 cards for four colors : 24 cards (76 + 24 = 100)
    4 draw 4 cards and 4 wild cards = 8 (76 + 24 + 8 = 108)

Componenets: 
    Player -> Handles player actions (Play or Draw or Auto Draw)
    Computer -> Handles computers action (Play or draw)
    Deck -> Handles the drawing of cards and verification of plays of Player and Computer
    Game -> Handles Menu printing and handles the above three componenets
